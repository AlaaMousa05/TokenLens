# Nimbus Cloud Backup — Subscription Policy

Nimbus is a cloud file-backup service. All plans are billed monthly on your
original signup date. No annual billing is offered.

## Plans

- **Lite** — $5/month. 100 GB of storage, 1 device.
- **Standard** — $12/month. 1 TB of storage, up to 5 devices.
- **Pro** — $25/month. 5 TB of storage, unlimited devices.

## Refunds

A new subscription is fully refundable within 14 days of its first charge.
After 14 days, the current billing period is non-refundable.

## Upgrades

An upgrade takes effect immediately. You pay the prorated difference for the
remainder of the current period, and that prorated upgrade charge has its own
14-day refund window.

Upgrading immediately ends the 14-day refund window on your original
subscription charge: once you upgrade, the original charge is non-refundable
even if fewer than 14 days have passed.

## Downgrades

A downgrade takes effect at the start of your next billing period. The current
period stays on the old plan at the old rate; no proration or refund applies.

## Cancellation

You may cancel anytime. Access continues until the end of the current paid
period. No partial refund is given for unused days, except when you cancel
within the 14-day new-subscription refund window, which is handled as a refund.
There is no cancellation fee.
